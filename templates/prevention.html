Lifestyle Changes

Stop Tobacco — #1 Priority

Tobacco causes 30% of all cancer deaths. Lung, throat, mouth, oesophagus, bladder, kidney, cervix, and stomach cancers are all directly linked. There is no safe level of tobacco use.

Action: Contact NTCP Helpline 1800-11-2356 for free quit support

Limit Alcohol

Alcohol increases risk of mouth, throat, oesophagus, liver, colorectal, and breast cancers. The risk increases with the amount consumed — less is always better. Even moderate drinking raises risk.

Action: Limit to 1 drink/day (women), 2/day (men) or better — eliminate

Exercise Regularly

Physical activity reduces risk of colorectal, breast, and endometrial cancers by up to 25%. It also reduces obesity — a key cancer risk factor. Exercise helps regulate hormones and inflammation.

Action: 150 min moderate or 75 min vigorous exercise per week

Maintain Healthy Weight

Obesity is linked to 13 types of cancer including breast, colorectal, pancreatic, kidney, and oesophageal. Excess body fat causes hormonal imbalances and chronic inflammation that promote cancer growth.

Action: Target BMI 18.5–24.9 through diet and exercise

Protect from UV Radiation

UV rays from the sun are the #1 cause of skin cancer (melanoma). India's proximity to the equator means high UV exposure. 90% of skin cancers are preventable with sun protection.

Action: Use SPF 30+ sunscreen, wear protective clothing, avoid peak hours (10am-4pm)

Improve Sleep & Reduce Stress

Chronic sleep deprivation disrupts hormones and immune function, increasing cancer risk. High cortisol from chronic stress suppresses natural killer cells that fight cancer cells.

Action: Target 7-9 hours of sleep nightly; practice yoga, meditation, or deep breathing

Diet & Nutrition

Eat More
  • Cruciferous vegetables — Broccoli, cauliflower, cabbage, kale. Contain sulforaphane that kills cancer cells.
  • Berries & citrus fruits — High in antioxidants that prevent DNA damage.
  • Legumes & whole grains — Fibre lowers colorectal cancer risk by 10–20%.
  • Fatty fish — Omega-3 fatty acids reduce inflammation linked to cancer.
  • Green tea — EGCG antioxidants may inhibit tumour growth.
  • Turmeric — Curcumin has documented anti-cancer properties in studies.
  • Tomatoes & carrots — Lycopene and beta-carotene are protective antioxidants.
Reduce or Avoid
  • Processed & red meat — WHO Group 1 carcinogen. Limit to <500g red meat/week, avoid processed meat.
  • Ultra-processed food — Chips, packaged snacks, instant noodles. Linked to 10% increased cancer risk.
  • Excess sugar — Feeds cancer cells and promotes obesity (a cancer risk factor).
  • Charred or burnt food — Produces heterocyclic amines linked to colorectal and stomach cancer.
  • Very hot beverages — Drinking tea/coffee above 65°C classified as Group 2A carcinogen (oesophageal cancer).
  • High salt food — Excess salt linked to stomach cancer. Use herbs instead.

Cancer-Preventing Vaccinations

HPV Vaccine

Prevents: Cervical, Anal, Throat, Penile cancers

Recommended for girls & boys aged 9–26. Most effective before sexual debut. Available in India as Gardasil/Cervarix.

₹2,000–4,000/dose · 2-3 doses required

Hepatitis B Vaccine

Prevents: Liver Cancer (HCC)

Free in India under National Immunisation Programme. Adults unvaccinated should get 3 doses. Hepatitis B is the leading cause of liver cancer in India.

Free under NIP · Private: ₹200–600/dose
